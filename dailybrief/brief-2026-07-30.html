AI / Coding Tools

Action todayGitHub Models retires today.↗
The playground, catalog, inference API and BYOK endpoints stop working for all users on July 30. Any repo or prototype still calling GitHub Models should be moved now to a supported provider such as Microsoft Foundry, direct model APIs, or Copilot-supported workflows.
Enterprise trendCLI coding agents showed a measurable adoption and output lift at Microsoft.↗
A study covering tens of thousands of engineers found adopters merged about 24% more pull requests over four months, while peer visibility strongly influenced adoption. The useful rollout lesson is to standardize a small number of approved tools, show working examples, and measure retained use rather than raw seat count.
Tool selectionDisney is reportedly consolidating around Codex, Claude and Cursor.↗
The reported move away from a broader tool set reflects a larger enterprise pattern: fewer overlapping coding assistants, more emphasis on developer preference, usage telemetry and measurable delivery speed.

Markets / Economy

MacroU.S. Q2 GDP slowed to 1.5%; inflation stayed above target.↗
Growth decelerated from 2.1% in Q1, while consumer spending remained solid and AI-related business investment stayed strong. Persistent inflation makes rapid rate cuts less likely, so long-duration assets and mortgage-sensitive sectors remain exposed to higher-for-longer rates.
RatesLong Treasury yields reached fresh multi-year highs after the Fed held rates.↗
The 30-year yield reportedly reached about 5.24%, reflecting concern about inflation and policy uncertainty. For near-term cash, attractive Treasury and money-market yields remain available; for equities, financing costs are becoming a more important valuation constraint.
