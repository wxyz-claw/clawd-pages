🩺 Health / Medicine

Phase 3Moderna/Merck personalized mRNA melanoma therapy hits Phase 3 endpoints ↗
The 1,137-patient study found intismeran + Keytruda significantly improved recurrence-free and distant-metastasis-free survival versus Keytruda alone after surgery for high-risk stage IIB–IV melanoma, with no new safety signal reported. This is the first positive late-stage result for an individualized mRNA cancer vaccine; full numerical data and regulatory review are still pending, so it is a major platform milestone rather than an immediately available treatment.
