Agents & Coding Workflows

Cloudflare-Security-Audit-Skill-Open-Source @RoundtableSpace: Cloudflare Open-Sources the Security-Audit Skill Behind Its Bug Harness
The skill that seeded Cloudflare's own vulnerability-hunting harness is out under MIT, with 31.3K stars, installable into a coding agent via `npx skills add ... --skill security-audit`. The six phases are the design worth copying: map the codebase, hunt bugs area by area so nothing is skipped, send every finding to a fresh agent that did not find it to confirm it is real, write structured results, verify each record again independently, then write the final report. Output is a findings file split into confirmed, needs validation and rejected, so real problems get fixed first.

Story2Board-Consistency-Layer-Storyboards @FanShifu: Story2Board Attacks Character Consistency in AI Storyboards
A former ByteDance team built a storyboard agent from 270,000+ annotated shots distilled out of 250 landmark films, and pointed it at the two things that break LLM storyboarding: faces changing between frames, and a single close-up reshoot resetting the whole art style. The fix is a Consistency Layer holding characters, wardrobe, signature props and sets as reusable assets, plus a shot-language model that recommends framing, axis, angle and camera movement from a plain-language scene description. Delivery is a printable PDF shot list with slug data, priced at $1 = 100 points with no subscription — which the author notes trades LTV and predictable MRR for a low barrier to try.

Town-108M-Series-B-Multiplayer-Ai @jgreze: Town Raises $108M Betting the Moat Is Multiplayer AI
Town announced a $108M Series B co-led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures, on the thesis that the real workplace bottleneck is waiting on each other rather than a lack of individual capability. The product gives every employee a Townie; Townies ask each other questions, share only what their person would share, and do the work, so usefulness compounds with how much the team actually collaborates. The claim to test is the moat: most assistants are built for one person, and work does not happen alone.

Elegant-Math-As-Human-Crutch @Noe1017_Monad: Elegant Mathematics as a Crutch for Human Working Memory
A provocative essay rather than a product note: elegant mathematical structures, the argument goes, exist because a top human mathematician can track only five or six interacting variables before collapsing, so we invent symmetry, invariants, homology and geometric intuition to avoid expanding high-order terms at all. Ten thousand concurrent agents, in this framing, can track ten thousand fifth-order cross terms as easily as 1+1 and simply expand to the seventh order, fit coefficients and cancel the divergent terms — no need to invent a concept a human can read. The bleak joke at the end: we finally got the answer from God's ledger and found he was not doing calculus, just pressing buttons on a calculator.
