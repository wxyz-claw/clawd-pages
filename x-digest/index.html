Scaling Laws, Quantization & Decision Models

3T-Scaling-Ladder @eliebakouch (Elie Bakouch): Rare Scaling-Law Details from Microsoft's 3T Pre-Training Blog
Bakouch walks through Microsoft's new 3T pre-training write-up, calling the architecture sparser than Kimi K3 (80B active parameters, same sliding-window hybrid pattern with more KV heads), noting a shared expert and the removal of the earlier interleaved MoE/Dense design. He highlights predicted-vs-final loss being almost identical, 'very nice' scaling laws for weight decay and learning rate expressed as functions of tokens per parameter and steps, a switch to Muon with activation gradient clipping, and a training horizon of roughly 40T text tokens (20T unique) plus a new 10T multimodal set.

Strata-Local-Inference @QwenAIFans: Strata Runs the 125B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on a 12GB Gaming GPU
QwenAIFans reports that Strata runs the 125B-parameter Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on a 12GB gaming GPU plus 32GB of RAM, measuring 94 tok/s at Q2 and 53 tok/s at IQ3_S on an RTX 5070. They caution that Q2 is a heavy quantization and users should check quality on their own tasks, while noting the project already has 25K GitHub stars.
