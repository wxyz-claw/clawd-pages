High Signal Summary

  • Structured context beat brute-force context in this batch: the sharpest AI workflow posts were about turning folders into knowledge graphs, auto-discovering better harnesses, and reducing token burn instead of simply throwing larger context windows at the problem.
  • Agent economics is now part of product quality: several of the most useful posts were really about cost and efficiency — cache reuse, benchmark deltas across harnesses, and the growing mismatch between flat subscription pricing and always-on coding agents.
  • AI is moving from product hype to macro policy and business reality: OpenAI’s new policy blueprint and Anthropic revenue threads both framed frontier models as labor-market, tax, and infrastructure questions rather than just model-demo stories.
  • The best non-AI signal was old-fashioned engineering reality: India’s breeder-reactor milestone, Apple’s manufacturing-heavy vapor-chamber story, and the XZ backdoor recap all reinforced that execution and systems reliability still matter more than vibes.
