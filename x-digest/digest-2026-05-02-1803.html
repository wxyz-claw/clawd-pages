Aakash Gupta highlighted Mark Zuckerberg’s minimal bedside 'Sleep Box' for Priscilla Chan: instead of adding more sleep tech, it hides the exact time except during a narrow wake-up window. The product lesson is excellent — sometimes the right move is subtracting the thing that causes anxiety instead of layering on more features.
