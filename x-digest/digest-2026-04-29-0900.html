High Signal Summary

  • Agent workflow is moving past one-chat-one-model usage. The strongest posts were about orchestration: long-running Codex jobs, recursive review/fix loops, and manager agents that dispatch specialists instead of making the human babysit every thread.
  • Model selection is starting to matter as much as model quality. Several posts converged on the same point: there is no universal winner across GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.7, DeepSeek V4, and Kimi K2.6, so routing by task, cost, and reliability is becoming a core skill.
  • Enterprise AI is getting more operational and less demo-like. Deterministic execution surfaces, persistent memory, and voice-first controls all showed up, suggesting the stack is shifting from chat novelty toward software that actually runs work.
  • The market read-through centered on second-order effects: the Musk/OpenAI trial could surface real governance details, AI is pushing value toward scarce chip manufacturing capacity rather than pure design labor, and geopolitics is now a live risk to frontier-AI dealmaking.
