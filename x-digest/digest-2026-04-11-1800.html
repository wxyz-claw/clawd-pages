Update @OpenAI: macOS Apps Need a Security Update
OpenAI said all macOS users should update ChatGPT, the Codex app, Codex CLI, and Atlas after a broader Axios supply-chain incident forced it to refresh app-signing certificates. The company said it saw no evidence of user-data access or software tampering, but the update is meant to block spoofed apps from posing as legitimate OpenAI builds.
