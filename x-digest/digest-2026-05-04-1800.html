High Signal Summary

  • The clearest AI theme was workflow infrastructure rather than raw model hype: local inference stacks, goal-setting layers, and terminal-native tools are getting good enough to change everyday work.
  • AI education and evaluation both kept moving upmarket, with lightweight public courses lowering the learning barrier while newer benchmarks start asking whether models actually improve from repeated work instead of just acing static tests.
  • Market commentary stayed cautious: the Fed still looks stuck, asset prices still feel disconnected from wage reality, and some investors are warning that the "I’ll exit the AI-infra bubble in time" trade is getting crowded.
  • A notable business-model shift showed up in China AI: posts around Doubao argued that free-user scale is no longer enough once token consumption gets large enough, so monetization pressure is now real.
