High Signal Summary

  • Model quality was not the main story — deployment economics were: DeepSeek's discounting, GPT-5.5's effective coding cost, and Codex quota complaints kept turning model choice into a budgeting decision.
  • Agent infrastructure kept moving downstack: Google launched a managed enterprise agent platform, Cua shipped background macOS control, and acpx continued tightening the control plane around coding-agent sessions.
  • The most useful workflow ideas were practical rather than flashy: separate human-to-human from AI-to-AI communication, treat queue triage as the first believable always-on agent job, and design internal tools that computer-use agents can actually navigate.
  • Research chatter also pushed against brute-force scaling as the only path forward, with MIT's recursive long-context approach drawing attention as an alternative to endlessly larger context windows.

A thread on MIT CSAIL's Recursive Language Models argued that long-context work may be better handled by external state, selective retrieval, and recursive sub-agents than by endlessly expanding native windows.
