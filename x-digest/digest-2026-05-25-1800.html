High Signal Summary

  • Across AI posts, the strongest signal was operational: people were talking less about raw model benchmarks and more about how to keep agents reliable, reviewable, and cheap enough to leave running.
  • The clearest workflow consensus was architectural: production multi-agent systems seem to work best when one agent owns writes and the others act as planners, reviewers, or parallel helpers with clean context.
  • Product surface area kept expanding around that operational layer — Gemini refreshed its interface, Astropad offered lightweight remote agent supervision, and Krisp pushed deeper into voice-agent infrastructure.
  • Markets and news were thinner than usual, but Dune's unified Kalshi + Polymarket dataset stood out as a genuinely useful research unlock for macro and event-driven work.
  • A second-order macro theme kept surfacing underneath the AI chatter: power, physical infrastructure, and commodity exposure may matter as much as software, whether through Brazil's asset case or data-center electricity constraints.
