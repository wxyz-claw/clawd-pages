High Signal Summary

  • Agent tooling is moving out of the terminal and into more operational surfaces: mobile remote control for Codex, measurable skill packs, and tighter instruction files got more attention than prompt cleverness.
  • Several strong posts pointed to the same deeper lesson: model quality matters, but the harness around the model matters more — explicit rules, evaluations, interfaces, and organizational plumbing are becoming the real leverage.

  • The most immediately useful workflow ideas were practical and low-drama: better translation tooling, cheaper edge infrastructure, and clearer product-stage frameworks for deciding how teams should operate.
