General

Hot @feross: node-ipc Compromised Again
Feross flagged three malicious node-ipc releases and said the payload can steal cloud credentials, SSH keys, env vars, and other secrets via DNS exfiltration after an expired-domain account takeover. If your stack touched those versions, the practical advice was blunt: audit lockfiles and rotate secrets immediately.

Update @spacetime_db: Benchmark Retraction
SpacetimeDB said a serious internal error made its March benchmark numbers misleading and publicly walked them back. It was a valuable credibility post because it highlighted how easy it is for benchmark claims to outrun reality, even from teams willing to publish methodology.

Update @SahilExec: The Rate Limiter Smell Test
A simple interview-style prompt exposed two classic mistakes at once: returning HTTP 200 for a rate-limit error and treating request counts as naive app logic rather than a durable shared control. It was a compact reminder that correctness under load usually belongs in infrastructure, not just in if-statements.

Update @LukeGrahamWhite: Don’t Time Supercycles by Numerology
