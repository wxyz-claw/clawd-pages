Update @chenchengpro: Anthropic's Speed Comes From Product Structure
A strong recap of Cat Wu's podcast explained that Claude Code's pace is driven less by magic and more by a system of low-approval launches, research-preview defaults, and constant removal of outdated prompt scaffolding as models improve. The useful operating idea is to prototype ahead of the models and let product judgment shift from process control toward taste and timing.
