Hot @LiuInTheShadows: China DRAM Supply Shock
A widely shared semiconductor thesis argued that Chinese DRAM expansion could break the current DDR5 scarcity story by H2 2027, citing former Samsung leadership and early signs of Chinese chips already entering western-branded modules. If that framing is right, one of the cleaner AI-adjacent trades, memory tightness, gets much less comfortable.

Update @linwanwan823: CRS 2.0 After Futu/Tiger
In the wake of the Futu/Tiger/Longbridge scare, this post argued that mainland users are underestimating the reporting side of the problem: CRS 2.0 plus CARF could make offshore brokerage and crypto activity far more legible to tax authorities. The actionable point is that compliance risk may now matter as much as market risk for China-linked accounts.
