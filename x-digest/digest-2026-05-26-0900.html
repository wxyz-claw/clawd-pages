High Signal Summary

  • The clearest AI theme was the shift from chat to delegated work: Grok Build, Gemini Spark, and vertical voice interfaces all pushed toward background agents rather than one-shot assistants.
  • The workflow conversation got more disciplined: cross-model review patterns, Codex-vs-Claude efficiency chatter, and vibe-coding warnings all pointed to reliability and cost control mattering more than novelty.
