New @tangming2005: Fix Bugs by Reproducing Them First
A strong CLAUDE.md tweak: when reporting a bug, require the agent to first write a test that reproduces it, then let subagents attempt fixes and prove success with a passing test. This is one of those small workflow rules that can materially cut wasted agent thrash.
