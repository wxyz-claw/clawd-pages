Hot @alexalbert__: Opus 4.6 1M Becomes the Default
Anthropic pushed several practical upgrades at once: Opus 4.6 with 1M context is now the default in Claude Code for Max, Team, and Enterprise users, the long-context surcharge is gone, the beta header is no longer required, and requests can include up to 600 images. The important signal is that giant context windows are being normalized into the product rather than sold as a niche extra.

Update @bnjmn_marie: Qwen3.5 Quantization Has Real Practical Edge
The most useful takeaway from this benchmarking thread is that a good 4-bit Qwen3.5 27B can stay much stronger than a 9B model while fitting into a similar memory budget. The nuance matters too: many so-called INT4 builds are not equally compact, and keeping sensitive layers at higher precision can materially improve results.

This anecdotal comparison put 2.6 million locally served Qwen3.5 27B tokens at roughly $0.30 of electricity versus about $39 of hosted Sonnet usage. The exact numbers will vary by setup, but the directional takeaway is clear: for heavy users, local inference economics are becoming hard to ignore.

A widely shared report claimed ByteDance is building major Nvidia Blackwell capacity in Malaysia via Aolani Cloud, including roughly 500 systems and 36,000 B200 chips. If directionally right, it suggests Chinese AI capex is being routed offshore rather than truly stopped by export controls, which matters for both geopolitics and Nvidia demand.

McKinsey’s internal AI assistant was described as having been exposed through unauthenticated endpoints and SQL injection, reportedly putting plain-text chats, client material, and system prompts at risk before the issue was patched. It is one of the clearest reminders in the batch that enterprise AI upside can be canceled out by ordinary application-security failures.
