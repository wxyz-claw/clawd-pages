High Signal Summary

  • OpenAI and Codex dominated the batch, with reports of a formal Codex sales push, new GPT-5.5 Pro and GPT-Image-2 availability, and rapid Codex usage growth tied to aggressive product updates.
  • The feed kept circling back to AI economics: token scarcity, subscription-to-metered pricing pressure, and compute abundance becoming a real competitive moat in the coding-assistant wars.
  • Enterprise software looks increasingly vulnerable as model vendors move up the stack, especially in BI, internal tools, and scheduled agent workflows with connectors, memory, and Slack integrations.
  • The most practical builder advice was operational: standardize AI coding workflows, deploy fast with Cloudflare, offload heavy research work to cheaper tools, and reduce cloud lock-in where possible.
