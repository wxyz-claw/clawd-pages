High Signal Summary

  • Browser automation got much more native: the clearest workflow shift in the batch was Chrome exposing the live browser session to agents, while agent-browser itself got much leaner and faster.
  • Long context is no longer a sufficient headline metric: the strongest AI posts focused on retrieval quality inside 1M-token sessions and on memory systems like OpenViking that structure context instead of dumping everything into one prompt.
  • AI tooling is maturing from hacks into repeatable workflows: model vendors are simplifying access to long context and browser control, which makes agent stacks easier to teach, ship, and operationalize.
  • The economic story is now about quality and org design, not just adoption: AI is helping generate a large share of shipped code, but review and debugging practices have not caught up, and frontier labs are being judged on execution as much as benchmarks.
  • Macro signal was light but defensive: the cleanest markets datapoint in the feed was continued gold ETF inflows, a reminder that geopolitical and policy uncertainty is still driving hedging demand.
