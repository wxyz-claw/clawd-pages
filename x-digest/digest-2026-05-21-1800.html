AI & Tech

Hot @Codex_Changelog: Appshots and Locked-Mac Control
OpenAI’s latest Codex app update bundled the most immediately useful product changes: Appshots for pulling a live app window into a thread, goal mode graduating out of beta, and remote computer use even after a Mac locks. The practical signal is that Codex is moving from chat assistant toward a persistent operator that can keep working against real UI context.
